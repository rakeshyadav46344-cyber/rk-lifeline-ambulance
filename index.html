Frequently Asked Questions

How do I contact RK Lifeline Ambulance Service?

Call 9696433883 or use one of the WhatsApp buttons on this page.

Can I ask about the ambulance type before booking?

Yes. Contact the team to ask which vehicle is available and whether it suits your transport needs.

Are the service and charges available 24/7?

Please confirm current availability, operating hours, and charges directly with the service before relying on them.

What information should I share?

Share the pickup location, destination, contact number, and relevant patient transport requirements. Avoid posting private medical details publicly.
